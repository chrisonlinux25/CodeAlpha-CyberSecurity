# Phishing Awareness Training

## About
An educational cybersecurity project designed to teach users how to
identify phishing emails, fake websites, and social engineering attacks.

## Topics Covered
- Phishing emails
- Fake websites
- Social engineering
- Smishing and vishing
- Common phishing indicators
- Prevention and reporting
